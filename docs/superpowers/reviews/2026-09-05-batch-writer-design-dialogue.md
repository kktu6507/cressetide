## Two material disputes resolved with evidence

**Semantic evidence carrier.** Claude initially opposed mandatory per-test evidence equality because he believed the closed record-kind/typed-ruling sets left no carrier, and suggested deferral or a spec amendment. Codex showed shared line 4 permits downstream implementation fields, shared section 2 defines *minimum* record payloads, and TP section 6 places `testRef` and body digests in the payload and names the full equalities. Existing store validation intentionally tolerates extra downstream fields. An independent append/validate/serialize/parse/validate experiment round-tripped all six fields as a normal untyped `review-ruling`. That experiment proves compatibility, not semantic validity. Claude inspected the passages and explicitly withdrew the deferral and amendment requirement in round 3.

The single carrier is `review-ruling`, `by: {kind: 'discipline', discipline: 'test'}`, `subjectRef` equal to the ASSUM clause, required `ruling`, and TP fields `taskId`, `testRef`, `baseBodyDigest`/`headBodyDigest` for actual sides only, `findingKind: 'assum-reading-change'`, and `binding`. No new kind, `rulingKind`, alternative spelling or hidden nesting. A `this-round` finding claiming this evidence must provide and match those fields to its task/result/entry/finding/transition. Missing fields refuse the claim. Evidence `baseBodyDigest` maps to result `observedBaseBodyDigest`; `taskId` must not be confused with `taskRef`. Generic old rulings retain their other valid uses and are not rewritten.

**Actual acknowledgement coverage.** Claude initially believed existing group coverage already proved the transition's acknowledgement link. Codex's accepted-reader-era experiment showed a group could name covering witness G while `Transition.ackRef` named another otherwise valid G2 with a wrong digest. Claude acknowledged the gap and proposed checking coverage on the actual ack plus requiring the same record identity as `governanceWitnessRef`; Codex adopted both. Claude then questioned plan-gate support. Shared section 2 already lists its conditional `resolutionGroupDigest`, and existing AC81 and AC60/65 REQ-mint fixtures provide it and use one record for both refs. Codex ran the existing AC60/65 test: 1/1 passed, exit 0. Claude inspected these sources and withdrew the contrary claim. The rule applies to all allowed witness kinds, without a plan-gate exception.

## Final writer and later consumer boundary

Writer checks required proposal/union shape, exact per-variant keys, inventory/result coverage, observations, derived equalities, captured CAS, reference resolvability, and conditional this-round evidence equality. `resolutionRef` is only for `assum-reading-change` and requires `finding.binding`. `T.subject` matches the binding clause. Claimed evidence for a newly minted transition belongs to that group's evidence set. Historical-convergence requires its closed union shape and a resolvable existing transition; no new base-capture input is invented.

Step 6 still owns full outcome/post-binding correspondence, mode-specific historical base-store membership and successor-chain proof, one authoritative source-freshness capture, and the convergence verdict. The existing producer and reader are accepted within their recorded scopes. Writer implementation, Step 6, artifact emission, Vigil, ledger, arbiter and final integration remain unaccepted. Phase 2 is not READY.
